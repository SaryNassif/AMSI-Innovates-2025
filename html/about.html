About EcoCitizens

EcoCitizens is a citizen science platform dedicated to empowering individuals in Dubai to report pollution issues they encounter. Our mission is to harness the power of community engagement and AI-driven validation to create a cleaner, more sustainable environment for all.

By submitting reports on pollution, users contribute valuable data that is analyzed and forwarded to the Dubai Municipality for action. This collaborative approach ensures that environmental concerns are addressed promptly and effectively.

Join us in making a tangible impact on our city's environmental health. Together, we can foster a culture of sustainability and responsibility, ensuring a greener future for generations to come.

Our Commitment to the Environment

At EcoCitizens, we believe that every individual has a role to play in environmental stewardship. Our platform not only facilitates the reporting of pollution but also educates the community on the importance of environmental awareness and sustainable practices.

We are committed to providing a user-friendly experience across both mobile and desktop platforms, ensuring that everyone can participate in this crucial initiative. Our AI-powered validation system guarantees that the data collected is accurate and actionable, leading to meaningful environmental improvements.

Together, let's embrace our responsibility to protect and preserve the environment. Your participation is key to driving positive change and ensuring a healthier planet for all.
